Frequently Asked Questions

Some useful details before starting your project.

Will you publish my website online?

Yes. Website deployment and launch assistance can be included in the agreed project scope.

Can you help me connect a custom domain?

Yes. We can help connect a domain you own. Domain registration and renewal fees are separate unless agreed otherwise.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

We design and test responsive layouts for mobile, tablet and desktop screens.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on the required pages, features, design and integrations. Contact us for a project-specific quote.

Will hosting be free forever?

Not necessarily. Some providers offer free plans, while others charge recurring fees. We clarify the hosting arrangement before the project begins.
